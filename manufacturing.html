Bringing discontinued parts back

From a measurement and a broken part to a finished replacement: CAD, prototyping, testing and production.

2025 – August 2026 · A completed chapter

My 3D printing business

Vehicle owners and machinery users kept running into the same wall: replacement parts that were discontinued or simply unavailable. So I founded an independent FDM 3D printing business serving clients in India and internationally.

  • Designed custom and replacement parts in Fusion for motorcycles, cars and obsolete machinery
  • Reverse-engineered discontinued components where no drawings existed
  • Handled quotes, sourcing, production, quality checks and shipping

"I learned how to identify a real market problem, develop a solution and deliver it to a real customer."

Rapid prototyping with ABS

Print settings tuned per material and per geometry, with parts designed for strength and manufacturability from the first sketch.

Acetone vapour smoothing

Every order shipped with a free acetone vapour-smoothed figurine, a small signature touch.

Self-taught CAD & FEA

I learned Fusion from scratch, and use FreeCAD FEA to check loads, constraints, stress distribution and deformation as part of every design iteration.

"I don't wait until I know everything before starting a project. I learn what I need, apply it, test it and improve."
