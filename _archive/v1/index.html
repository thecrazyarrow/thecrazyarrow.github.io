01 // about

About Me

I'm Aaron, an engineering generalist with a BTech in Electronics & Communication from the College of Engineering Perumon (CEP), Kerala.

I've always been the person who takes things apart to understand how they work, and then builds something better from the pieces. My work sits where electronics, mechanical design and digital manufacturing meet: if it can be modelled, printed, wired, machined or rebuilt, I want to make it.

I don't use templates. Every project starts from a blank canvas, and "Engineered by Chaos" is the philosophy behind it: ideas get messy, prototypes break, and the chaos is where the real engineering happens.

Outside the workshop, I ride, I film, and I founded and ran the Anime Club at CEP for four years.

Sci-Fi 3D-Printed Electric Guitar
