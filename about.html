About

An engineer who learns by building

I'm Aaron, an electronics & communication engineer from the College of Engineering Perumon in Kerala, living in Kuwait. I learn by building: when something doesn't exist, I design it; when something breaks, I take it apart until I understand it.

My approach is always the same: identify a problem, learn what's needed, design, build, test and improve. That loop took me from a cement 3D printer to a print business that brought discontinued parts back, and through engines, lathes and circuit boards along the way.

Outside the workshop there are the bikes, Ava and Toraa, and the guitar, where my first song is on its way. The chaos is what drives all of it. Next stop: an engineering career in Japan.
